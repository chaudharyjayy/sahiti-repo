About Sahiti

A clearer path for everyday business.

Sahiti is a multilingual business companion for entrepreneurs, farmers, and small businesses. It brings practical planning tools into one calm, easy-to-understand place so you can explore an idea, understand the numbers, and decide what to do next.

01

Plan with context

Start with your location and business idea. Sahiti helps you think about demand, costs, risk, and realistic growth in language that feels close to everyday life.

02

Check the numbers

Use the loan calculator, ROI tracker, market check, and document guide to understand possible next steps before you commit time or money.

03

Grow with confidence

Sahiti is designed to make complex choices feel more manageable, with plainspoken guidance and tools that keep the important numbers visible.

Please read before using Sahiti

Terms & Conditions

1. Prototype service. Sahiti is an early-stage prototype created to demonstrate business-planning and financial-literacy tools. Some login, OTP, calculations, content, and recommendations may be simulated or incomplete.

2. General information only. Sahiti does not provide a loan, guarantee approval, or replace advice from a qualified financial, legal, tax, or business professional. You are responsible for checking information and making your own decisions.

3. Your information. Please do not enter passwords, payment details, confidential business documents, or sensitive personal information into the prototype. Use only information you are comfortable using for testing.

4. AI assistant limits. The assistant is intended for Sahiti business topics and approved in-session tools. Its responses may be incomplete or inaccurate; review important figures and decisions independently.

5. Changes and availability. Features may change as Sahiti develops. The service is provided for demonstration and testing, without a promise that every tool will always be available or error-free.
